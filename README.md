## The entry

One word: **blackhole**.

The boss who swallows your time, your weekends, your energy and your credit —
and gives nothing back. A single common word, funny on its own, true of a
habit every worker has met, fresh enough that it is not the first word anyone
reaches for, and it travels from a kitchen to a warehouse to an office without
needing an explanation.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes exactly that one word — 9 characters, no spaces,
no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, inside the competition's gate of
1–30 UTF-8 characters.

Run it locally:

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
